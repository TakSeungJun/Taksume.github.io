About me

My research focuses on LiDAR SLAM, 3D scene representation, and robust spatial mapping for autonomous robots. In particular, I am interested in real-time LiDAR-based localization and mapping using Gaussian representations, dynamic-scene understanding, and long-term mapping in changing environments.

My broader research interests include LiDAR SLAM, 3D Gaussian Splatting, dynamic and lifelong mapping, 3D reconstruction, localization, and robot perception.

Research Interest

Driven by a deep interest in Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and 3D scene representation, I am currently researching advanced SLAM methodologies capable of robust performance in dynamic environments with moving objects and changing environments.
